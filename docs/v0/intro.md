Outline is a place to write notes, organize your work, and find what you need. It is available on macOS and iOS. The guides here focus on the macOS app.

Your notes are files in folders called **Spaces**. They stay on your device or in the storage location you choose, so you can work with the files directly. You can write with the editor, add tables and drawings, and link related notes.

Outline also lets AI agents work with notes you choose to share. You can use [Outline Chat](/docs/v0/ai/starting-an-ai-chat) or [connect an external AI app](/docs/v0/ai/mcp-with-ai-clients). AI is optional; you can start by writing a note.

[Get Started](/docs/v0/get-started) walks you through your first note.
