---
title: "Create and manage Spaces"
description: Create a Space, add an existing folder, and switch between Spaces on your Mac.
---
Spaces keep different sets of notes together. You can start with the Space Outline provides, make a new one, or use a folder you already have.

## Create a Space

1. Open the Space selector at the top of the sidebar and select **New Space**.
2. Enter a **Space name** and leave **Type** on **Create new**.
3. Under **Location**, choose **iCloud** to make the Space available on your other Macs, or **This Mac** to keep it on this Mac. iCloud appears when it is available.
4. Select **Create space**.

For how iCloud availability and other Macs work, see [Store Spaces in iCloud](</docs/v0/spaces/icloud>).

## Use an existing folder

1. Open the Space selector and select **New Space**.
2. Under **Type**, choose **Use existing folder**.
3. Select **Choose folder**, then pick the folder containing your notes.
4. Optionally change the Space name, then select **Create**.

Outline reads and saves notes in the selected folder. It does not move that folder. Its location and any syncing remain under your control.

## Switch or open a Space

Select the current Space name at the top of the sidebar, then select another Space. To work in two Spaces at once, open the same menu and use a Space's **…** menu to select **Open in New Window**.
