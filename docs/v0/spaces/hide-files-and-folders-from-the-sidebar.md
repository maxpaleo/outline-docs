Hide a file or folder to clear it from your sidebar while keeping it available in your Space. Hidden items remain available to search, links, open tabs, and AI agents. Hiding a folder also hides everything inside it.

## Hide an item

1. Right-click the file or folder in the sidebar.
2. Select **Hide**.

## Show and unhide items

1. Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Show Hidden Items**. Hidden items have an eye-off icon.
2. Right-click a hidden item and select **Unhide** to show it normally.
3. Select **Hide Hidden Items** when you want to stop displaying other hidden items.
